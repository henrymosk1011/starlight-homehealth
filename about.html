About Starlight

Caring for Southern California families since 2015.Caring for Southern California families since 2015.

Starlight Home Health Services is a multilingual home health agency in Burbank and part of Starlight Health System, Inc. Since 2015, we have cared for homebound patients across Los Angeles, San Bernardino, Ventura and Orange County.

Our mission

Why we do this work.

To provide innovative, quality and cost effective home health services to homebound individuals who need skilled nursing, rehabilitation and support services, in the comfort of their own home.

Experience has shown that recovery from illness is favorably influenced when health care is provided in the familiarity of one's own residence, surrounded by family, friends and personal comfort. Our staff has over 15 years of experience providing quality home health services.

Our care team respects each individual patient's ethnic, religious and cultural background. Because of this, we provide care that honors differences and accommodates the diverse population we serve.

What guides us

Four promises we keep at every visit.

These principles shape how we hire, how we train and how we care for patients and families.

  • Privacy and dignity

    From bathing to wound care, every service respects each person's privacy and dignity and is tailored to individual needs.

  • Clinical excellence

    Highly trained nurses, therapists and physicians use the latest technology and techniques to ensure the best possible outcomes.

  • Respect for every background

    We honor each patient's ethnic, religious and cultural background, and care for the diverse population we serve.

  • Multilingual care

    As a multilingual agency, we help patients and families understand every part of the care plan.
