Who we are

Recovery from illness goes better when care happens in the familiarity of your own home, surrounded by family, friends and personal comfort.

Starlight Home Health Services is a multilingual home health agency providing 24 hour, cost effective home health services to homebound individuals who need skilled nursing, rehabilitation and support services at home.

Our care team respects each patient's ethnic, religious and cultural background, and provides care that honors differences and serves our diverse community.

Getting started

Four steps to care at home.

We handle the coordination with your doctor and your insurance, so you and your family can focus on getting better.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Talk with your doctor

    Home health begins with a physician's order. Ask your doctor, hospital or discharge planner to refer you to Starlight, or call us and we will help coordinate.

  2. 02

    Step 2: We confirm your coverage

    Our intake team verifies your insurance benefits and answers your questions before care begins.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Your first visit

    A clinician visits your home to review your health, home safety and personal goals.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Your plan of care

    With your physician, we set a care plan and schedule visits from the right nurses and therapists.
