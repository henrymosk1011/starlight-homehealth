For referral partners

Refer a patient to Starlight.Refer a patient to Starlight.

We make it simple for physicians, hospitals, skilled nursing facilities and discharge planners to continue care at home.

Send a referral

Fax referrals to (844) 269-6817.

Questions about a referral? Call our intake team at (818) 849-6044, Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm.

Include with each referral

  • Face sheet with insurance information
  • Physician order for home health services
  • History and physical, plus recent progress notes
  • Current medication list
  • Diagnoses and any wound care or therapy orders
