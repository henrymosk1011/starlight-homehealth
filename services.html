6 services

Nursing and medical care

Clinical care that would otherwise mean a trip to the hospital or clinic.

  • Skilled nursing

    Registered and licensed nurses manage medications, monitor health conditions, teach patients and families, and keep the physician informed at every step of care.

  • Wound specialist and surgical site care

    Wound and surgical site care is an important service offered by our home health team. Our highly trained nurses and clinicians are experienced in providing specialized treatment and care to patients with wounds and surgical sites. We provide comprehensive assessments and care plans to ensure the best possible outcomes.

  • IV administration

    Our experienced nurses administer medications, fluids and nutrition directly into the body to help ensure your health and wellbeing. Our dedicated professionals make sure every IV treatment is safe and effective.

  • Mobile physician

    Our mobile physician service provides quality medical care in the comfort of your own home. Experienced physicians use the latest technology to diagnose and treat a variety of medical conditions, so you can access quality healthcare without ever leaving home.

  • Portable X‑ray and ultrasound

    Convenient and reliable imaging for patients in the comfort of their own home. Our experienced healthcare professionals are trained to deliver exceptional imaging with the latest technology, tailored to each patient to ensure the best possible outcome.

  • Blood and urine testing

    Comprehensive blood and urine testing at home supports accurate diagnosis and treatment. Our highly qualified staff use the latest technology and techniques to conduct tests and provide results quickly and accurately.

4 services

Therapy and rehabilitation

Licensed therapists help patients rebuild strength, skills and confidence.

  • Physical therapy

    Our physical therapy service provides comprehensive treatment tailored to each patient. Our highly trained therapists help patients regain mobility, reduce pain and improve overall health, so they can reach their fullest potential and live healthier, more active lives.

  • Occupational therapy

    Occupational therapy helps individuals of all ages achieve independence in daily activities such as self care, work and leisure. Our therapists create customized treatment plans to improve physical and cognitive abilities, so patients can live life to the fullest.

  • Speech therapy

    Comprehensive assessment and treatment of communication difficulties. Our highly trained, experienced therapists help patients with articulation, voice, fluency, language and swallowing disorders.

  • Registered dietitian

    One of our most unique services: a registered dietitian who provides individualized nutrition counseling and education, with a personalized nutrition plan that helps each patient maintain a healthy, balanced lifestyle.

4 services

Support at home

Practical help and equipment that make home a safe place to recover.

  • Oxygen and respiratory equipment

    We deliver oxygen tanks and other respiratory equipment to your home quickly and efficiently, so you have the support you need. Our team of certified healthcare professionals is available to answer any questions or concerns you may have.

  • Podiatrist

    Our podiatrists provide specialized care for your feet, diagnosing and treating a wide range of conditions affecting the lower limb, including corns, calluses, ingrown toenails and arthritis. Our goal is to help you maintain healthy, comfortable feet.

  • Bath and wash services

    Professional, quality care for those who need help with bathing and washing. Our experienced nurses provide a safe and comfortable experience that respects each person's privacy and dignity, tailored to individual needs.

  • Durable medical equipment (DME)

    High quality durable medical equipment helps you maintain your independence and quality of life. We offer an extensive selection, from wheelchairs and walkers to hospital beds and other assistive devices, and our knowledgeable staff will help you find the right solution for your situation.
