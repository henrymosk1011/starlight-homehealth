Accessibility

Accessibility statement.Accessibility statement.

Starlight Home Health Services is committed to a website that everyone can use, including people with disabilities.

Our standard

We design and test this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, the standard referenced by the Americans with Disabilities Act and Section 508.

Accessibility features

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a clearly visible focus indicator.
  • A skip link at the top of each page jumps straight to the main content.
  • Text and controls meet or exceed WCAG color contrast requirements.
  • Pages reflow on small screens and support text zoom up to 200 percent.
  • Headings, landmarks, labels and alternative text are structured for screen readers.
  • The Pause animations switch at the top of every page stops all motion, and the site follows your device's reduced motion setting automatically.
  • Forms use clear labels, describe errors in text and never rely on color alone.
  • Navigation, contact details and help options appear in the same place on every page.

Language assistance and auxiliary aids

Language assistance and auxiliary aids are available to patients and families. Call (818) 849-6044 to request help in your preferred language or format.

Feedback and help

If you have trouble using any part of this website, please tell us. Call (818) 849-6044 or email starhhcare@gmail.com. We will provide the information you need in another format and work to fix the issue.
